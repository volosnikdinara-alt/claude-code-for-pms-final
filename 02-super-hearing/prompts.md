### 11.
Turn the biggest mismatch between the interviews and the tickets into a problem statement I can drop into a PRD. Include who’s affected, evidence from both sources with counts and quotes, what we’d get wrong by trusting only one source, and success metrics that would show the problem is solved.

### 12.
Focus on what’s frequent in the tickets but never mentioned in interviews. For each issue, estimate the support cost (ticket volume × typical handling effort) and say whether it’s a product fix, a docs fix, or an onboarding fix. Rank by cost saved per unit of engineering effort.

### 13.
For each theme that shows up in only one of the two sources, estimate how big the opportunity is. Use ticket volume as a proxy for pain and interview frequency as a proxy for desire. Classify each theme as high pain/high desire, high pain/low desire, low pain/high desire, or low pain/low desire. Tell me which quadrant deserves roadmap space and why.

### 14.
Based on the interviews and the tickets, what’s the single biggest problem our customers have? Back it up with numbers and one example from each source.

### 15.
Based on the interviews and the tickets, what’s the #1 issue and how should we resolve it? Give me the issue, the evidence from both sources, the root cause, the recommended fix, the effort, and the metric that will show it worked.
